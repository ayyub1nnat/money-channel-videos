# Privacy Policy — Simple Money Life Uploader

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

Simple Money Life Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel **Simple Money Life** to upload and manage that channel's own videos.

**What it accesses.** With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, translations and playlists, and post comments on the owner's own channel. It does not access any other user's data.

**What it stores.** It stores only the OAuth credentials needed to act on the owner's channel, kept privately and never shared or sold. It does not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or any other people.

**Third parties.** Data is sent only to Google/YouTube through the official YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube data is subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Revoking access.** The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ayyub1nnat/money-channel-videos/issues
